Two of the three photography tiers. Pick by the job the image does.

```jsx
<Figure src="assets/worldskills-lyon-2024.jpg" alt="WorldSkills Lyon 2024"
  position="50% 22%" caption="WorldSkills Lyon, September 2024. Evidence keeps its colour." />

<MoodImage src="assets/burj-view.jpg" alt="Looking out over the Dubai skyline" />
```

`Figure` keeps full colour because it proves something. `MoodImage` is grayscale until hovered and takes no caption. For the chrome tier (portraits, avatars) use `Avatar`.
