Use `Avatar` wherever a portrait or a mark would go. There is no logo in this system.

```jsx
<Avatar src="assets/burj-view.jpg" alt="Mikael Ribeiro" size={56} />
<Avatar src="assets/burj-view.jpg" alt="Mikael Ribeiro" size={132} />
```

The defaults are tuned for `burj-view.jpg`. For a different source photograph, adjust `scale`, `offsetX` and `offsetY` until the face sits on the centre, then record the values.
